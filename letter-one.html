Letter One

The Beginning

☾

Dear friend,

Have you ever wondered why people look toward heaven when they have nowhere left to go?

I used to think heaven was a place. Somewhere above the clouds, beyond the stars, beyond everything that had ever hurt us.

A place where every question would finally find its answer, and every lost thing would be waiting exactly where we had left it.

Perhaps I was wrong.

Or perhaps I simply wanted to believe that the sky was kinder than the world beneath it.

I don't know when this letter will reach you. I don't even know if letters can find the people who are no longer here to receive them.

Still, I have to begin somewhere.

So let this be the first page. Not of the truth, perhaps, but of my attempt to find it.

Until the next page,
Someone who still looks up.
